Wednesday 8 July 2026

A strong week for UK AI, led by compute and physical AI: Nscale's £670m credit facility for data-centre buildout, HIVE's £11.2m seed for its industrial 'silicon brain', Worldmodeldata's £7m stealth exit and Luffy AI's neuroplastic-AI Series A. Companies & rounds

London hyperscaler Nscale secures £670m credit facility UKTN

London-based AI hyperscaler Nscale has secured a $900m (£672m) revolving credit facility from a syndicate of major investment banks to fund its data-centre buildout — fresh capital into UK sovereign compute capacity.

HIVE secures £11.2m to build ‘silicon brain’ for industrial machines UKTN

HIVE, a physical-AI company building a 'silicon brain' intelligence layer for industrial machines, raised a $15m (£11.2m) seed round; it claims its system cuts the hourly cost of running machines by up to 80%.

Worldmodeldata raises £7m to accelerate gaming data for AI training UKTN

Worldmodeldata came out of stealth with a £7m seed round to turn video-game data into training data for physical AI, adding an ex-Meta policy VP to its board.

Oxfordshire-based Luffy AI raises €9.4 million Series A to scale neuroplastic AI for real-time adaptive control EU-Startups

Oxfordshire-based Luffy AI raised a €9.4m Series A to scale its 'neuroplastic' AI for real-time adaptive control — novel-architecture territory squarely in the Fund's frontiers.

Ecosystem

EU offers quota deal to end UK-France standoff over €5bn Scaleup fund Sifted

The EU has offered a quota compromise to end the UK-France standoff over the €5bn Scaleup Europe fund, which will determine UK companies' access to the pot.

UK spinout funding just had its worst year on record, and AI is soaking up the money Tech Funding News

UK university spinout funding hit its worst year on record even as AI absorbs a growing share of capital — a structural warning for the deep-tech pipeline the Fund draws on.

Andy Burnham's plan to overhaul AI strategy sparks backlash Sifted

Andy Burnham's plan to overhaul the UK's AI strategy has sparked backlash across the sector; he is separately expected to review Palantir's NHS contract. The direction of UK AI policy is now visibly contested.

UK start-ups double fundraising to $17bn amid AI boom The Times

UK startups raised $17bn in H1 2026 — the strongest half since 2022 — with AI taking 74% of all venture capital and a record 44% of small-business equity deals (HSBC / British Business Bank data).

Landmark review of AI in finance presents warning of risk UKTN

The FCA published the landmark Mills Review on agentic AI in retail financial services, warning of risks — effectively setting the regulatory direction for AI in UK finance.

UK IPO market shows early signs of revival UKTN

The London Stock Exchange recorded seven new listings raising £577m in H1 2026 — early signs of an IPO-market revival, and a healthier exit environment for UK scale-ups.
